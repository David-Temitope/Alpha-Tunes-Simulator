# Alpha Tune - Play Store Compliance Guide

## App Information
- **App Name**: Alpha Tune
- **Package Name**: com.alphatune.musicgame
- **Category**: Simulation Games
- **Content Rating**: Teen (13+)
- **Target SDK**: 34 (Android 14)
- **Min SDK**: 21 (Android 5.0)

## Privacy Policy Requirements

### Data Collection
The app collects and stores:
- User-created artist profiles (name, email, genre)
- Game progress and statistics
- In-app purchase history (if applicable)
- Device storage for save files

### Data Usage
- All data is stored locally on the user's device
- No personal data is transmitted to external servers
- Game progress is saved to device internal storage
- No analytics or tracking without explicit consent

### Permissions Required
- **WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE**: Save game progress to device
- **READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE**: Load saved game data
- **INTERNET**: For future online features (optional)
- **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**: Check connectivity status

## Content Guidelines Compliance

### Music Industry Simulation
- All content is fictional and educational
- No real artist names or copyrighted content
- Promotes positive values through tithe system
- Encourages hard work and dedication

### Monetization (Optional)
- In-app purchases for cosmetic items only
- No pay-to-win mechanics
- All core gameplay accessible without purchases
- Clear pricing and purchase confirmations

### Age Appropriateness
- No violent or inappropriate content
- Positive moral messaging
- Educational value about music industry
- Suitable for teens and adults
