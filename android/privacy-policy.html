Privacy Policy for Alpha Tune

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Information We Collect

Alpha Tune is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information necessary for game functionality:

  • Game Data: Your artist profile, game progress, and statistics
  • Device Storage: Save files stored locally on your device
  • Optional Data: Email address for artist profile (stored locally only)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Save and restore your game progress
  • Provide personalized gaming experience
  • Maintain game statistics and achievements

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • No personal information is transmitted to external servers
  • Game data is encrypted and secured
  • You can delete all data by uninstalling the app

4. Permissions

Alpha Tune requests the following permissions:

  • Storage Access: To save and load your game progress
  • Internet Access: For future online features (optional)

5. Third-Party Services

Alpha Tune does not integrate with third-party analytics or advertising services. All functionality is self-contained within the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Alpha Tune is rated for ages 13+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@alphatune.game
Website: www.alphatune.game

9. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data (view in-game profile)
  • Delete your data (uninstall the app)
  • Modify your data (edit in-game profile)
  • Export your data (contact support)

10. Data Retention

Your game data is retained locally on your device until you choose to delete it or uninstall the app. We do not have access to or store your data on external servers.
